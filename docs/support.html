About BI Splitter

BI Splitter is an iOS app that makes splitting restaurant bills effortless. Add people, scan receipts, browse the restaurant's menu, assign items, handle birthdays, sync in real time across phones at the table, calculate tax and tip, export professional PDFs, and request payment — all from your iPhone.

Getting Started

  • Restaurant — Enter a name (required to save) or tap 🔍 to search Apple Maps. Selecting a result auto-fills both the name and address for you.
  • Add people — Type a name in the People section and tap +, import from Contacts, or load a saved Contact Group. Tap Save as Group (teal button) to save the current list for future bills.
  • Birthday? — Tap the Birthday capsule on a person's row. Their share becomes $0 and is absorbed by the rest of the group automatically.
  • Exclude from birthday share — Tap the Exclude capsule on a person's row to mark them with ⊖. Excluded people only pay their own consumed items and are not asked to cover the birthday person's share.
  • Add items — Enter an item name and price, then tap the people who had it. The consumer grid shows all names without scrolling — just tap to toggle checkmarks. Items shared by multiple people are split equally.
  • Browse Menu — Once a restaurant name is set, tap Browse Menu in the Items section header. BI Splitter fetches the restaurant's menu using AI, showing items with descriptions, prices, dietary tags, and Popular badges. Tap an item to select it, then tap Add Selected to add them all at once.
  • Scan a receipt — Tap ··· → Scan Receipt to use the camera with on-device OCR, or Attach Photo to pick from your camera or gallery.
  • Live Sync — Tap the 📡 antenna icon in the toolbar to let everyone at the table edit the same bill in real time over local WiFi/Bluetooth.
  • Review the summary — Scroll down to see each person's itemised share, including tax and tip.
  • Export or share — Use the PDF export button to save a professional two-page receipt, or Share Bill to send the full bill to another device.
  • Save the bill — Tap the save icon (↓) in the top right. You'll need to enter a restaurant name first.
  • Default tax & tip — Go to the Settings tab to set your preferred default tax and tip percentages. Every new bill starts with those values pre-filled.
  • Go Pro — Tap Unlock Pro in Settings for a one-time purchase that unlocks the Not Paying feature, saved Payment Defaults (Zelle/Venmo/Cash App), and custom PDF Branding (logo + header color).

Features in Detail

🍴 Restaurant Address & Map

The address field is optional. Tap the magnifying glass icon to open a search sheet pre-seeded with the restaurant name — results come from Apple Maps. Selecting a result auto-fills both the restaurant name and address directly from the map, so the names always match. Once an address is saved, the icon changes to a map pin; tapping it opens Apple Maps to that location.

👥 Adding People & Contact Groups

Three ways to add people:

  • Manual — type a name (and optional phone number) then tap +.
  • Contacts (blue button) — pick one or more people from your address book.
  • Add Group (indigo button) — import all members of a saved Contact Group in one tap. Anyone already on the bill by name is skipped automatically.

When the bill has people on it, a teal Save as Group button appears. Tap it to name and save the current list as a new Contact Group for future reuse. Manage groups on the Groups tab.

🎂 Birthday Person

Tap the Birthday pill on any person's row to flag them. You can flag multiple people.

  • Their share becomes $0 — their items' costs are redistributed to the non-birthday consumers.
  • If an item was ordered only by birthday people, its cost is split among all non-birthday, non-excluded people.
  • The 🎂 emoji and pink highlight appear on their row; payment buttons are hidden since they owe nothing.
  • Birthday indicators appear in the Share Bill preview, on both pages of the exported PDF, and in the Saved Bills list.
  • Tap Remove Birthday to unmark them.

⊖ Exclude from Birthday Share New

Sometimes one or more people at the table don't want to chip in for the birthday person's share — for example a plus-one or someone who arrived late. Tap the Exclude capsule on their row to mark them with ⊖.

  • Excluded people pay only the items they personally consumed — their fair base share is never redistributed.
  • The birthday person's costs are absorbed only by the remaining (non-excluded) people.
  • You can combine Birthday and Exclude freely: birthday people still pay $0; excluded people pay only their own items; everyone else covers the birthday share.
  • The ⊖ badge and an "own items only" note appear on their row and on both pages of the exported PDF so the split is transparent to everyone.
  • Tap Remove Exclude to undo at any time.

↪ Not Paying Pro

Sometimes one person's share needs to be covered by a specific other person — a parent covering a kid's meal, one friend treating another — rather than split evenly across the whole table. Tap the Not Paying capsule on a person's row to set this up.

  • If Pro isn't unlocked yet, tapping the capsule opens the paywall so you can purchase or restore it.
  • Once unlocked, tapping it opens a picker where you choose exactly one other person on the bill to cover the full share — items, tax, and tip.
  • The flagged person shows $0.00 and a purple "Paid by [name]" note; the designated payer's own total increases by that exact amount.
  • Not Paying is mutually exclusive with Birthday and Exclude on the same person — turning one on clears the others.
  • You can't pick someone who is themselves flagged Not Paying as the payer, so shares can never chain through multiple people.
  • Removing a person from the bill automatically clears any Not Paying assignment that pointed to them, so totals never silently fall out of balance.
  • The ↪ indicator and "Paid by" note appear on the person's row, on both pages of the exported PDF, and in the Share Bill preview.

🤖 Browse Menu New

Once a restaurant name is set, tap the Browse Menu button in the Items section header. BI Splitter uses AI to look up the restaurant's menu and returns a rich item list with:

  • Name & price — ready to add directly to the bill.
  • Description — a short summary of each dish.
  • Category chips — filter by Appetizers, Mains, Desserts, Drinks, etc.
  • ⭐ Popular badge — highlights the restaurant's best-known dishes.
  • Dietary tags — 🌱 Vegetarian, 🌿 Vegan, GF Gluten-Free, 🌶 Spicy.
  • Search — type to filter by item name or description.

Tap items to select them (checkmark appears), then tap Add Selected to add them all to the bill at once. If the restaurant address is set, BI Splitter uses it alongside the name to fetch the correct location's menu. Menu results are cached for the session — switching to a different bill or changing the restaurant name fetches fresh results, but browsing the same restaurant a second time is instant.

🧾 Assigning Items to People

In each item row, all people on the bill are displayed as a wrapping grid of toggle buttons — no horizontal scrolling needed. Tap a person's chip to add or remove them from an item's consumers. A checkmark indicates they're assigned. The cost is split equally among all assigned consumers. If no one is assigned, the item is split among all non-birthday people.

⚙️ Default Tax & Tip New

Go to the Settings tab and find the Bill Defaults section. Use the steppers to set your preferred default tax percentage (0–25%, in 0.5% steps) and default tip percentage (0–40%, in 1% steps). Every new bill you create will start with these values pre-filled, saving you from adjusting them on every bill. You can still change the tax and tip on any individual bill at any time.

📄 Professional PDF Export Updated

Tap the PDF export button to generate a two-page professional receipt:

  • Page 1 — A branded header with the restaurant name, address, and date. A participant list with 🎂 (birthday), ⊖ (excluded), and ↪ (Not Paying) indicators. A three-column items table (Item | Shared By | Price) with alternating row stripes. A totals block showing subtotal, tax, tip, and a highlighted Grand Total bar.
  • Page 2 — Individual per-person breakdown cards showing each person's items, tax, and tip share. Birthday people show "Covered by group 🎉" in pink. Excluded people show "own items only" in orange. Not Paying people show "Paid by [name]" in purple. Zelle, Venmo, and Cash App payment information (if entered). The attached receipt photo (if any).

PDF Branding Pro — In Settings → PDF Branding, upload a logo and pick a custom header color. Both appear on every exported PDF in place of the default navy header, on both pages.

📡 Live Sync (Nearby Devices)

Everyone at the table can edit the same bill simultaneously — no internet required.

  • Tap the antenna icon in the Bill tab toolbar to start syncing. It turns green and pulses while searching for nearby devices.
  • Nearby iPhones running BI Splitter with sync active will be discovered automatically over WiFi or Bluetooth.
  • When a device is found, the other phone receives an invitation: "[Device] wants to join your live bill session." Tap Accept to connect.
  • Any edit (adding people, items, birthday flags, tip changes) is broadcast to all connected phones within 300 ms.
  • A green banner shows who is connected. Tap ✕ in the banner or the antenna icon again to stop syncing.

📷 Receipt Scanning & Photo Attachment

  • Scan Receipt — uses the camera with on-device OCR to detect item names and prices automatically. Review and correct any misread items before adding them to the bill.
  • Attach Photo — attach an image of the receipt. Choose Take Photo (camera) or Choose from Gallery (photo library). The attached photo appears on page 2 of the exported PDF.

💳 Payments

  • Request — tap the green Request button on a person's row to open Venmo's charge-request screen pre-filled with the amount, or a pre-written iMessage if Venmo isn't installed.
  • Birthday and Not Paying people show $0 with no Request button — they owe nothing.

To tell people how to pay you back, enter your Zelle email/phone, Venmo @username, and/or Cash App $cashtag in the People section — these print on page 2 of the exported PDF. Payment Defaults Pro — save them once in Settings → Payment Defaults and every new bill pre-fills them automatically.

📂 Saved Bills & History

  • Every bill's total amount is shown next to the restaurant name in the list.
  • Use the search bar to filter by a person's name. Matching bills show that person's individual share for each bill.
  • Bills with birthday people show a pink 🎂 line with the birthday person's name.
  • Tap a bill to load it into the Bill tab. Swipe left to share via deep-link. Swipe right to delete.

📊 Analytics

  • Over Time — line chart of total bill amounts filtered by 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or all time.
  • Top Spenders — ranked list and bar chart showing total spend, number of bills appeared in, and average spend per bill for each person. Birthday shares ($0) are correctly excluded so rankings aren't skewed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the tip calculated?

By default, tip is calculated on the subtotal before tax (Pre-Tax Tip). Toggle the "Pre Tax Tip" switch in the Summary section to change this behaviour.

How is each person's share calculated?

Each person pays for the items assigned to them. If an item is shared by multiple people, its cost is split equally. Tax and tip are distributed proportionally based on each person's item total. Birthday-flagged people always pay $0 — their portion is absorbed by the remaining non-excluded people.

How does Birthday Mode split the costs?

Items the birthday person consumed are redistributed to the other consumers of those same items. If an item was only ordered by birthday people, its full cost is split equally among all non-birthday, non-excluded people. The grand total of the bill is unchanged — it's just redistributed.

What does "Exclude from Birthday Share" do exactly?

Marking someone with ⊖ means they only pay for the items they personally consumed — they are not asked to help cover the birthday person's share. For example, if someone joined late and only had dessert, they can be excluded so they pay just for dessert while the rest of the table covers the birthday share. Excluded people's own item costs are never redistributed to others.

Can I combine Birthday and Exclude on the same bill?

Yes. Birthday people pay $0 as usual. Excluded people pay only their own items. Everyone else (neither birthday nor excluded) shares the cost of the birthday person's items. All combinations are supported — including multiple birthday people and multiple excluded people on the same bill.

What does "Not Paying" do, and how is it different from Exclude? Pro

Exclude means a person pays only their own items and contributes nothing extra. Not Paying goes further: that person pays nothing at all, and you choose exactly one other person on the bill to absorb their entire share (items, tax, and tip) — instead of it being spread evenly across everyone. It's a Pro feature, and it's mutually exclusive with Birthday and Exclude on the same person.

Can I chain Not Paying — have my payer's share covered by someone else too? Pro

No. When picking who covers a Not Paying person's share, anyone already flagged Not Paying themselves is excluded from the list, so shares can never chain through multiple people.

What happens to a Not Paying assignment if I remove the payer from the bill? Pro

BI Splitter automatically clears that Not Paying assignment so the bill's totals stay accurate — the person reverts to paying their own share until you pick a new payer.

How does Browse Menu work?

Once a restaurant name is entered, tap Browse Menu in the Items section header. BI Splitter sends the restaurant name (and address if available) to an AI model which returns a list of likely menu items with prices, descriptions, and dietary information. Results are approximate — always verify prices against the actual menu. Browse Menu requires an internet connection.

Does Browse Menu call the internet every time I open it?

No. Menu results are cached for the current session. If you open Browse Menu again for the same restaurant, the previously fetched results are reused instantly — no extra API call is made. The cache is cleared when you start a new bill or change the restaurant name.

Browse Menu prices look wrong — what should I do?

AI-generated menu data is a best-effort estimate and may not match current prices exactly. Always double-check against the restaurant's actual menu or your receipt. You can edit any item's price directly in the bill after adding it.

How do I set a default tax and tip percentage?

Go to the Settings tab (gear icon) and find the Bill Defaults section. Use the steppers to set your preferred tax (0–25%, 0.5% steps) and tip (0–40%, 1% steps). New bills will open with those values pre-filled. You can still adjust tax and tip on any individual bill at any time without affecting the default.

How does Live Sync work? Does it need the internet?

No internet required. Live Sync uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework to connect nearby iPhones directly over WiFi or Bluetooth. All data stays on-device and on your local network — nothing is sent to any server. Both phones must have sync active (antenna icon enabled) and be within WiFi/Bluetooth range (typically the same room).

What happens if two people edit the bill at the same time during sync?

Changes are broadcast with a short 300 ms debounce. The most recent change wins — if two people edit simultaneously, the last change received by each phone becomes the shared state. For best results, take turns on large edits like adding many items at once.

How do I search for a restaurant's address?

In the Restaurant section of the Bill tab, tap the 🔍 magnifying glass icon next to the address field. A search sheet opens pre-filled with the restaurant name. Tap any result to auto-fill the restaurant name and address from Apple Maps. Once saved, tap the 🗺 map icon to open it in Apple Maps.

How do I save the current people on a bill as a group?

Once you've added at least one person to the bill, a teal Save as Group button appears in the People section. Tap it, give the group a name, and tap Save. The group appears in the Groups tab and is available for future bills via the indigo Add Group button.

Will adding a group duplicate people already on the bill?

No. When you add a Contact Group, anyone whose name already appears on the bill is automatically skipped. Only new names are added.

Can I share a bill with someone who doesn't have the app?

The Share Bill deep-link feature requires the recipient to also have BI Splitter installed. To share with anyone, use Export PDF instead — it generates a formatted two-page receipt that can be sent via Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or any other app.

What's on each page of the exported PDF?

Page 1 shows the restaurant name and address, date, participant list (with 🎂 for birthday people, ⊖ for excluded people, and ↪ for Not Paying people), a formatted items table, and a totals block with subtotal, tax, tip, and grand total. Page 2 shows each person's individual share breakdown with items, tax, and tip columns, Zelle/Venmo/Cash App payment info (if entered), and the attached receipt photo (if any). Pro users can also add their own logo and header color — see PDF Branding below.

Why does receipt scanning sometimes miss items?

Receipt OCR works best on flat, well-lit receipts with clear printed text. Try scanning again with better lighting, or add items manually. You can also use Attach Photo to store a photo of the receipt on the bill without OCR parsing.

Where is my bill data stored?

All bill data is stored locally on your device using Core Data. It is never uploaded to any server (except restaurant name and address when using the Browse Menu feature — see our Privacy Policy). Live Sync data travels only over your local network between connected devices and is never stored externally.

How do I delete a saved bill?

Go to the Saved Bills tab and swipe left on any bill to reveal the Delete option. If the list is filtered by name, deletion still correctly removes the bill from your full history.

How do I load a saved bill to edit it?

Tap any saved bill in the Saved Bills tab. It will load into the Bill tab so you can make changes and re-save it.

How do I filter saved bills by person?

Pull down on the Saved Bills list to reveal the search bar, then type a person's name. The list narrows to bills that include that person, and each row shows their individual share for that bill.

How do I see who spent the most across all bills?

Go to the Analytics tab and switch to the Top Spenders view. Each entry shows the person's total spend across all saved bills, how many bills they appeared in, and their average spend per bill.

How do I request payment from someone?

In the person's row, tap the green Request button. BI Splitter will open Venmo's charge-request screen (if installed) pre-filled with the amount, or send a pre-written iMessage to the person's phone number.

What are the Zelle, Venmo, and Cash App fields for?

You can optionally enter your Zelle email/phone, Venmo @username, and/or Cash App $cashtag in the People section. This information is printed on page 2 of the exported PDF so recipients know where to send your payment. With Pro, save them once in Settings → Payment Defaults so every new bill pre-fills them automatically — you can still edit or clear them per bill.

How do I unlock Pro, and what does it cost? Pro

Go to the Settings tab and tap Unlock Pro (or tap any Pro feature directly, like the Not Paying capsule). It's a single one-time purchase — no subscription — that unlocks Not Paying, Payment Defaults, and PDF Branding for good. The exact price is shown on the purchase screen before you buy.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I unlock it here? Pro

Open the purchase screen (Settings → Unlock Pro) and tap Restore Purchases. BI Splitter checks Apple's own purchase records for the Apple ID signed into this device — no account or sign-in with us is needed.

How do I add my own logo or color to the PDF? Pro

Once Pro is unlocked, go to Settings → PDF Branding. Tap Choose to pick a logo image from your photo library — it's automatically resized — and use the color picker to set a custom header color. Both appear on every exported PDF going forward. Tap Remove or Reset to Default Color to revert to the original navy branding.

How do I export all my bills?

In the Saved Bills tab, tap Export Bills in the top-left corner to download a JSON file of all your saved bills — useful for backup or transferring to a new device.

How do I use Contact Groups?

Go to the Groups tab to create and manage reusable lists of people. Tap + to create a group, give it a name, and add members from your Contacts. When adding people to a bill, tap the indigo Add Group button to instantly load all members. You can also save a bill's current people as a new group using the teal Save as Group button in the Bill tab.

The app is asking for Contacts permission — is that required?

No. Contacts access is entirely optional. You can always add people manually by typing their names. If you denied access and want to enable it later, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Contacts and toggle BI Splitter on.

The app is asking for Local Network permission — what is that for?

This permission is required for the Live Sync feature, which connects nearby iPhones over your local network so everyone at the table can edit the same bill. If you don't use Live Sync, this permission has no effect on the app.
